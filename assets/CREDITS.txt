Version 0.4 adds more Medieval Hexagon buildings to the town (barracks, tavern, mine, castle, windmill,
watermill, church, market, blacksmith, towers, bridge, scaffolding, fences, crates and trees), also by

Music and sound effects: generated in code by the game itself (original, no samples).
Game design, code, UI and icons: original work for Hollowmere.

---- Version 0.6 additions ----
Realistic characters: built at runtime with the ara3d library (MIT licence, (c) Joshua Olatunde, https://github.com/olatunde85/3d-gen),
vendored as src/vendor/ara3d.js. The character data in assets/ara/ is derived from MakeHuman (http://www.makehumancommunity.org),
explicitly released under CC0 1.0 by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier (see assets/ara/LICENSE.txt).
Character animations: motion capture from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu), retargeted by
ara3d. "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."
Music: "Battle Theme A" by cynicmusic (https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-a), CC0 1.0, used for guardian fights.
All other music and sound effects are generated in code. Blood, gore, skills, perks, zodiac stars and barks are original work.

Owner-supplied music (shipped in the installer and the owner's web build at the owner's request):
 - Chopin, Nocturne No. 19 in E minor, Op. 72 No. 1 (town at night)
 - Emile Mosseri, "Jacob's Prayer" (Minari soundtrack) (town by day, title menu)
 - "Lobotomy" by unseenglobalmedia (title menu fallback, every dungeon floor)
The owner is responsible for holding the rights to distribute these recordings.

---- Version 0.7 additions ----
More Medieval Hexagon buildings and props (colour variants of taverns, markets, churches, smithies, towers, mills, barracks,
wells, stages, crates, flags) and house furniture from KayKit Dungeon Remastered (beds, tables, chairs, shelves, kegs, plates,
bottles, candles, trunks, banners, wooden floors, walls, doors and windows), all by Kay Lousberg under CC0 1.0.
Building interiors, townsfolk routines and the expanded town layout are original work.

---- Version 0.7.7 additions ----
Skill and spell emblems: the shapes are icons from game-icons.net (https://game-icons.net) by Lorc, Delapouite and other
contributors, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The game extrudes them into 3D relief
medallions at runtime (src/skillicon3d.js); the original vector shapes are in assets/icons/skill-icons.json.
Sound effects: layered sample files (assets/sfx/*.ogg) from the Kenney audio packs (https://kenney.nl), CC0 1.0, mixed with the
game's own synthesised sounds.
Alien enemies (Grey Wretch, Grey Zapper, Void Grey, Abyssal Grey, The Pale Visitor): the alien character model, rig and texture were
supplied by the owner (itch.io, GamesFromUnderground, "alien retro PS2 style model"). The owner is responsible for holding the rights to
distribute this model. Its walk, run, attack, cast, hit and death animations were authored for Hollowmere.
HOLLOWMERE title logo and installer / app icon: supplied by the owner.
Title cinematic, 3D emblem renderer, walk-speed matching and all new gore, barks and difficulty settings: original work.
